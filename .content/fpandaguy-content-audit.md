# TheFPandAGuy.com - Content & E-E-A-T Audit Report

**Audit Date:** November 12, 2025
**Site:** www.thefpandaguy.com
**Primary Service:** FP&A Training, Courses, Certifications, Software Consulting

---

## Executive Summary

TheFPandAGuy.com is positioned primarily as an **educational platform** (courses, certifications, podcasts) rather than a traditional B2B professional services firm. The site demonstrates strong topical authority in FP&A education but lacks critical B2B service elements including client case studies, portfolio examples, and detailed consulting service pages.

**Overall E-E-A-T Score: 6.5/10**

## Conclusion

TheFPandAGuy.com has a **strong foundation as an educational platform** but requires significant enhancements to function effectively as a **B2B professional services website**. The site demonstrates Paul Barnhurst's genuine expertise and thought leadership but lacks the trust signals, portfolio evidence, and service clarity that enterprise buyers require.

**Biggest Opportunities:**
1. Position consulting services more prominently alongside education offerings
2. Add essential trust and compliance elements (privacy policy, case studies, testimonials)
3. Create detailed service pages with clear engagement processes and pricing
4. Build portfolio showcase with sample deliverables
5. Develop decision-stage content targeting buyers ready to hire

**Biggest Risks:**
1. Missing privacy policy creates legal liability and unprofessional appearance
2. Lack of case studies and client testimonials undermines credibility for consulting
3. 404 errors on key pages signal incomplete or abandoned site sections
4. Absence of pricing transparency may deter qualified prospects
5. Education-heavy positioning may obscure consulting services availability

**Expected Impact of Recommendations:**
- E-E-A-T score improvement from 6.5 to 9.0 within 6 months
- Consulting inquiry rate increase 200-300% with clearer service pages
- Organic search traffic increase 150-200% with content gap filling
- Conversion rate improvement 50-100% with trust signals and clear CTAs
- Authority and referral growth through thought leadership initiatives

With focused execution on the roadmap above, TheFPandAGuy.com can evolve from a solid education platform to a high-converting B2B professional services site that attracts quality consulting clients while maintaining its educational mission.
