## The Offer (What Members Get)

### Core Deliverables

1. **Weekly Group Call (4x/month)**
   - Live Q&A, deal strategy, buyer coaching
   - All calls recorded for replay
   - 60 minutes each
   - Tuesday evenings at 7pm PST

2. **Deal Reviews (2x/month)**
   - Live teardowns of member listings
   - Real numbers, real analysis
   - Learn by watching others' deals

3. **Monthly Expert Session (1x/month)**
   - Guest from lending, legal, or operations
   - Live AMA format
   - Practical dealmaking insights

4. **In-Depth Personal Deal Review (1x/month)**
   - Each member can submit one deal
   - Custom Loom video breakdown
   - James's full analysis and recommendations

5. **Monthly Training Drop (1x/month)**
   - Tactical guide or video
   - Topics: Evaluating seller claims, structuring earnouts, red flags, etc.

6. **Private Community (Always-On)**
   - Share deals, ask questions, connect with peers
   - James engages several times per week

7. **Resource Vault (Updated Quarterly)**
   - Deal checklists
   - Buy box templates
   - Outreach scripts
   - Due diligence tools

### 3-Month Prepay Bonus

**$525 for 3 months ($175/month effective) includes:**
- 1:1 Buy Box Build Session with James
- Private 60-minute call to define:
  - Target business type and size
  - Budget and capital plan
  - Deal flow strategy
