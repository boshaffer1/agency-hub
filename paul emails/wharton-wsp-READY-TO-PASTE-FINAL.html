Frequently Asked Questions

How does the cohort-based learning work?
You'll join a cohort of 30-40 professionals for 8 weeks. There are weekly office hours, group discussions, and collaborative projects. It's designed to build both skills and professional networks.
What's the difference between this and other Wharton programs?
This is specifically designed for FP&A professionals and combines Wharton's strategic frameworks with Wall Street Prep's practical modeling skills. It's more focused than an MBA but more comprehensive than typical online courses.
Can I work full-time while taking this program?
Yes, the program is designed for working professionals. However, you'll need to dedicate 8-12 hours per week, including some scheduled live sessions. Most participants manage this alongside their full-time roles.
Is financial modeling experience required?
Basic Excel skills are expected, but you don't need advanced modeling experience. The program teaches modeling progressively, building from fundamentals to advanced techniques.
How does this compare to getting an MBA?
This is much more focused and practical than an MBA, specifically targeting FP&A skills. It's also significantly less expensive and time-consuming. However, an MBA provides broader business education and different networking opportunities.
