Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Wharton/WSP FP&A Certificate cost?
The program costs $4,950 USD. This includes all course materials, live sessions with instructors, peer collaboration, and the certificate from Wharton Executive Education.
How long is the Wharton/WSP FP&A program?
The program runs for 16 weeks with 4-6 hours of commitment per week. It includes live sessions, recorded content, hands-on projects, and peer collaboration.
What makes this program different from other FP&A certifications?
This is a cohort-based program with live instruction from industry experts, peer networking, and the prestigious Wharton brand. It focuses on strategic FP&A leadership rather than just technical skills.
Who should take the Wharton/WSP FP&A Certificate?
This program is ideal for experienced finance professionals (5+ years) looking to move into senior FP&A roles, current FP&A managers wanting strategic skills, and ambitious analysts ready for leadership positions.
Is financial modeling experience required?
Basic Excel and financial knowledge is expected, but advanced modeling skills are not required. The program teaches practical FP&A skills progressively, building on your existing foundation.
