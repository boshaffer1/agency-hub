## KEYWORD STRATEGY SUMMARY

### Priority Tiers for Implementation

**Tier 1 - Immediate Focus (Q1 2025)**
- High-intent service keywords (#1-15): Core service pages
- Top industry-specific (#46-50): SaaS/tech niche positioning
- Essential problem-solution (#16-20): Content marketing foundation

**Tier 2 - Secondary Development (Q2 2025)**
- Remaining problem-solution keywords (#21-30)
- Top thought leadership (#31-40)
- Remaining industry-specific (#51-60)

**Tier 3 - Long-term Authority Building (Q3-Q4 2025)**
- Advanced thought leadership content
- Template library expansion
- Tool/calculator development

### Content Format Recommendations

**Service Pages** (10-12 pages needed)
- Keywords #1-15, #46, #48, #57
- Include: Services, pricing models, ROI calculator, case studies, CTA

**Ultimate Guides** (8-10 pillar pages)
- Keywords #16, #17, #20, #28, #31, #36, #51
- 3,000-5,000 words, comprehensive, template downloads

**Technical Tutorials** (15-20 articles)
- Keywords #21-27, #37-44, #47, #52-54, #56, #58-60
- 1,500-2,500 words, step-by-step, visual aids

**Tools & Calculators** (5-7 interactive tools)
- Keywords #22, #30, #38, #50, #55
- Lead magnets with service CTAs

**Case Studies** (6-8 industry-specific)
- Support keywords #46, #48, #51, #56, #57, #59
- Problem → Solution → Results format

### SEO Technical Recommendations

1. **Site Architecture**
   - /services/ (high-intent keywords #1-15)
   - /industries/ (industry-specific #46-60)
   - /resources/guides/ (problem-solution #16-30)
   - /knowledge/ (thought leadership #31-45)
   - /tools/ (calculators/templates)

2. **Internal Linking Strategy**
   - Thought leadership → Service pages
   - Problem-solution → Consultation CTAs
   - Industry guides → Industry service pages
   - Tool pages → Discovery calls

3. **Conversion Funnel**
   - Awareness (guides/thought leadership) → Email capture
   - Consideration (comparison/tools) → Free audit/consultation
   - Decision (service pages) → Discovery call booking

4. **Local SEO Enhancement**
   - Add location modifiers: "Utah fractional CFO", "Salt Lake City FP&A consultant"
   - Regional case studies
   - Local business schema markup

**Low Competition Opportunities (Quick Wins)**
- Keywords #24, #25, #29, #33, #34, #40, #42-44, #50, #52-54, #56-60
- Target these first for faster ranking

**High Value, High Competition (Long-term Investment)**

**B2B Decision-Maker Focus (70% of strategy)**

- Include pricing transparency where possible
- Case studies with measurable outcomes

**Practitioner Content (Authority Building - 30%)**
