FPAP (FP&A Professional) is the digital self-study program covering core technical skills—modeling, Excel, budgeting, forecasting. FPAP is ideal for entry to mid-level FP&A professionals (0-5 years experience) or those transitioning into FP&A from other finance roles. It focuses on building strong technical foundations.
